## Privacy Policy

**Effective date**: 2026-10-07
**Built by**: CodesbyFebin (codesbyfebin@gmail.com)

### Data We Collect

Deproof is a local-first application. All contribution data, receipts, and configuration are stored on your device in a local database. Deproof does not collect, transmit, or store personal data on any external server operated by the developer.

**Data stored locally**:
- DePIN contribution observations and timestamps
- Portable contribution receipts (evidence envelopes)
- Wallet public key (after user-initiated wallet connection)
- Node configuration you provide

### Data We Do Not Collect

- Private keys or wallet seed phrases (never accessed)
- Analytics or usage telemetry
- Crash reports sent to the developer
- Location data outside the app (location is used locally for proof observations only)

### Third-Party Services

- **Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter**: When you connect a wallet, the wallet app (Phantom, Solflare, etc.) handles all key operations. Deproof only receives your public key.
- **Solana RPC**: When live RPC features are enabled, transaction queries are sent to the configured Solana RPC endpoint. No personal data is included in these queries.
- **DePIN Networks**: Adapter integrations (Helium, Hivemapper, etc.) are currently in BLOCKED status. No data is sent to these networks without your explicit action.

### Your Rights

You can delete all locally stored data by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in Android Settings.
