EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
DEPROOF is an open-source Android + Web3 application for decentralized transaction verification, evidence integrity, and phased ecosystem participation. Built by CodesbyFebin for Solana Mobile and DePIN networks.

Tagline: Own your node. Prove your contribution.

Core promise: Verifiable trust through hardware-backed proof, transparent local records, and independently verifiable results.

150 requirements organized across 6 workflows and 5 phases (P1–P5), with 86 function contracts and 100 acceptance checks governing all behavior.
